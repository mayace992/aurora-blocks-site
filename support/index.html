Contact

Email mayace992@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, game version and a short description of the issue. Screenshots or a screen recording are welcome.

Progress and records

Game progress, settings and personal records are stored locally on the device. Deleting the app also deletes this local data.

Ads and audio

Audio is mixed with music and video playing on your device. Advertising requires a network connection and may be unavailable temporarily.
